About Pixie

Pixie is a private, owner-operated AI assistant used by Andy Bochmann through OpenClaw. It is not offered as a public service. This policy describes the assistant’s Google integration and this informational website.

Information accessed

After Google authorization, Pixie can access the account’s identity and email address; calendar and event information; email messages and metadata; task lists and tasks; and read-only contact information. Its authorized permissions also allow calendar changes, sending email, and task changes. Pixie processes the owner’s requests and information returned by the tools used to fulfill them. Permissions describe available access, not a claim that all account data is downloaded.

How information is used

Information is used to answer the owner’s questions, summarize relevant information, organize schedules and tasks, and perform authorized actions. Google data is not sold or used for advertising. Pixie’s use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Processing and sharing

Pixie uses configured AI providers, including OpenAI, to interpret requests and generate responses. Relevant Google data may be included in the context sent to those providers. Responses containing that information may pass through the owner’s configured messaging or voice providers. When the owner instructs Pixie to send an email or share an event, the corresponding information is sent to the selected recipients.

These services process information under their applicable terms and account settings. Local operation of OpenClaw does not mean that all processing stays on the owner’s computer. This policy does not promise zero retention by external providers.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are stored in the owner’s operating-system credential store and are not included in this website. Requests, tool results, responses, and selected information may be retained in assistant conversation history, logs, memory, or backups. No universal automatic deletion deadline is specified for this owner-operated setup. Copies may remain until the owner deletes them or the applicable system’s retention process removes them. External providers apply their own retention settings and policies.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The owner can revoke Pixie’s Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access using that authorization, but does not automatically erase previously stored conversations, logs, memory, messages, or backups. The owner can remove stored credentials and local copies separately and use external providers’ deletion controls where available. Questions or requests about retained information can be sent to the contact below.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics scripts, advertising, forms, Google sign-in, or application-set cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, when serving the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting the site does not grant access to a Google account.

Contact and changes

Contact Andy Bochmann at andy.bochmann@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when relevant data practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
