A personal assistant

A little help with everyday life.

Pixie is Andy Bochmann’s private AI assistant, operated through OpenClaw to help with personal organization and everyday requests.

Google integration

With the owner’s Google authorization, Pixie can:

  • Read and manage calendar events.
  • Read email and send messages when instructed.
  • Read and manage tasks.
  • Read contacts to help identify people and recipients.

Pixie can summarize information and answer questions through the owner’s configured chat and voice interfaces. Relevant information may be processed by configured AI and communication service providers to fulfill these requests.

Personal use only

Pixie is not a public sign-up service. This website provides information about the assistant; it does not provide access to the assistant, connect visitors’ Google accounts, or display private Google data.

Read the privacy policy for information about data access, use, storage, sharing, and revoking access.
